Questions travel agency owners usually ask before working with us

Everything you need to know before applying for the Admospheria 5-Day Performance Trial.

What is the 5-Day Performance Trial?

The 5-Day Performance Trial lets you experience our Meta Ads strategy and optimization process before committing to a long-term partnership. During the trial, we launch and optimize campaigns for your travel agency to generate qualified enquiries.

Is the trial really free?

Yes. We do not charge any service fee during the 5-day trial. You only pay your Meta advertising budget directly to your own Meta Ad Account.

Do I pay the ad budget to Admospheria?

No. Your advertising budget is paid directly to Meta from your own Ad Account. We only get partial access to manage and optimize your campaigns.

Who is eligible for the trial?

The trial is available only for Indian travel agencies that are ready to invest a minimum monthly Meta Ads budget of ₹20,000 and are genuinely looking to generate quality travel enquiries.

What if my monthly ad budget is below ₹20,000?

At the moment, we work only with agencies that meet our minimum advertising budget requirement because it allows us to properly test, optimize, and scale campaigns.

What types of travel agencies do you work with?

We work with domestic and international travel agencies, honeymoon package providers, group tour operators, customized holiday planners, pilgrimage tour operators, and destination-specific travel businesses across India.

What happens after I submit my application?

We review your application to understand your agency, destinations, and advertising readiness. If your agency qualifies, we'll contact you within 24-48 hours via Call or WhatsApp.

Will I receive genuine travel enquiries instead of random leads?

Our landing page and qualification process are designed to filter low-quality enquiries before submission. While no campaign can guarantee only perfect leads, our focus is on generating higher-intent travel enquiries compared to basic forms.

What is included during the 5-day trial?

Your trial includes Meta Ads campaign setup, audience research, campaign launch, Meta Pixel integration, daily optimization, and a performance review at the end of the trial.

Do you guarantee bookings?

No. We don't guarantee bookings because bookings depend on factors like pricing, sales follow-up, package quality, and customer decisions. Our role is to generate and optimize qualified travel enquiries through Meta Ads.

What happens after the trial ends?

We'll share the campaign performance and discuss the next steps. If you're satisfied with the quality of enquiries and results, we can continue managing and scaling your campaigns.
